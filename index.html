Power Prestation — Study Abroad & Academic Mobility Consulting in Yaoundé, Cameroon

Power Prestation is an academic and professional mobility consultancy based in Yaoundé, Cameroon. We guide students and professionals through every step of studying and working abroad — from choosing a university to landing in the destination country.

Our services

  • University and programme selection for study abroad
  • Scholarship application support for African students
  • Student and work visa guidance for international students
  • Internship and professional placement assistance
  • Complete admission file preparation and review

Destinations we support

We help Cameroonian and other African students apply to institutions in Canada, France, Germany, Belgium, and beyond, including scholarship and student visa procedures for each destination.
